# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-03-05

This Privacy Policy describes how **Data Engineering Smart & Hard Way — LinkedIn Scheduler** ("the App") collects, uses, and stores information.

## What the App does
The App allows a user to schedule and publish text posts to LinkedIn using LinkedIn's official APIs.

## Data we store
We store the minimum data needed to provide scheduled posting:

- Scheduled post content (text)
- Scheduled publish time
- Publish status and attempt logs (success/failure, timestamps)
- LinkedIn author identifier (author URN)
- LinkedIn OAuth access token (stored securely)

## How we use data
We use stored data only to:
- Publish posts to LinkedIn at the scheduled time
- Retry failed posts up to a limited number of attempts
- Maintain an audit trail for debugging

## Sharing of data
We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with third parties except:
- **LinkedIn**, to publish content you requested

## Data retention
Scheduled posts and logs may remain stored until you delete them or request deletion. Tokens can be revoked by you at any time.

## Security
We take reasonable steps to protect stored data and credentials. Access tokens are treated as secrets and should not be exposed publicly.
